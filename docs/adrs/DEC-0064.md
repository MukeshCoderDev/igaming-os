# DEC-0064 — Q1–Q5 Increment 1+2 Non-Blocking Acceptance

## Decision

Accept Q1 (jurisdictions), Q2 (licensing), Q4 (pilot), Q5 (budget) as
non-blocking for Increments 1 and 2 engineering (Phases 1–14), subject
to the conditions enumerated in `docs/phase-0-evidence.md` §Decision.

## Rationale

The audit chain has treated Q1–Q5 as BLOCKED carry-over since Phase 1.
Resolving them requires external validation (counsel, pilot partner,
funder) that is outside the engineering critical path for Increments 1
and 2.

The architectural baseline (RLS, audit chain, outbox, state machine, KYC
framework, consent framework) is designed to accommodate Q1–Q5
resolution without rework — jurisdiction-override mechanisms,
stub-adapter patterns, and ASSUMED-status flags are in place.

## Conditions

1. All Q-dependent ADRs carry status ASSUMED or OPEN-DEPENDENT until
   the relevant Q is resolved.
2. No regulated decision (AML/CFT, consent, retention, jurisdiction)
   may transition to ACCEPTED until Q1 is resolved.
3. Phase 22 (first customer onboarding) requires Q1, Q2, Q4, Q5
   resolved.
4. Phase 11 (Ledger & FX) requires Q5 (FX vendor) resolution OR
   explicit stub-adapter acceptance (this DEC, amended).

## Supersession

This DEC supersedes the chronic "Q1–Q5 BLOCKED carry-over" treatment
that has been implicit across Phases 1–10 audits. From DEC-0064 onward,
Q1–Q5 are not "BLOCKED" — they are "OPEN-DEPENDENT with documented
acceptance for Increments 1 and 2."
