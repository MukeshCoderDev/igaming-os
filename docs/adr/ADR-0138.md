# ADR-0138 — Journey Orchestration — Multi-Step Flows & Exit Conditions

## Context

Journeys are multi-step campaign flows. A journey might be: "Welcome series — Step 1: welcome email (immediate); Step 2: deposit prompt email (24h delay); Step 3: bonus offer SMS (48h delay); Step 4: re-engagement push (7 days delay)." Players can exit mid-journey for multiple reasons: self-exclusion (Law 18), unsubscribe, cap exceeded, consent revoked, terminal step reached.

Without explicit exit conditions, excluded players continue receiving journey steps — a Law 18 violation. The 2022 UK GC incident (cited in ADR-0137) included a journey failure: a player self-excluded after Step 1 but the journey continued sending Steps 2, 3, and 4 because the journey enrollment had no exit hook for self-exclusion. The regulator called it "systemic journey integrity failure."

The 2023 Caesars Entertainment enforcement (£2M) was similar: a player unsubscribed after Step 1 of a 5-step journey; the operator's journey engine did not check suppression at step execution; the player received Steps 2-5 despite unsubscribing. The fix is explicit exit conditions + per-step MarketingGate check (ADR-0140) + enrollment status transitions that capture exit reasons.

**Linter rule 202:** every journey enrollment has `exit_reason` NOT NULL when `status=exited`.

### Journey execution

1. **Enrollment:** player joins segment → enrolled in journey (`status=active`); `current_step=1`; `next_step_at = enrolled_at + step_1.delay_minutes`.
2. **Step execution:** at `next_step_at`, MarketingGate checks eligibility (queue-time check per ADR-0140):
   - If eligible: send queued; on send, MarketingGate checks caps + consent (send-time check per ADR-0140).
   - If denied at queue-time: enrollment `status=suppressed`; `exit_reason` recorded; journey continues for other players.
   - If denied at send-time: send skipped; step may retry (configurable; OQ-ENG-003) or advance.
3. **Step advancement:** after send (or skip), `current_step++`; `next_step_at = now + next_step.delay_minutes`.
4. **Terminal step:** after last step, enrollment `status=completed`; `exit_reason=terminal_step`.

### Exit conditions (Law 18)

- **Player self-excludes mid-journey** (Phase 14 kill-switch): enrollment exits; `status=exited`; `exit_reason=self_excluded`. The kill-switch fan-out (Phase 14) calls `JourneyService.exit_enrollment(player_uuid, reason='self_excluded')` in the same transaction as the self-exclusion.
- **Player unsubscribes** (suppression list): next send denied at queue-time; enrollment exits; `exit_reason=unsubscribed`.
- **Player enters cooling-off** (Phase 14): next send denied at queue-time; enrollment exits; `exit_reason=cooling_off`.
- **Player reaches terminal step**: `status=completed`; `exit_reason=terminal_step`.
- **Player revokes consent** (Phase 9): next send denied at send-time; enrollment exits; `exit_reason=no_consent`.
- **Player DSAR-erases** (Phase 21 ADR-0135): enrollment `player_uuid` NULLed; journey continues anonymized for analytics (no player-attributable data retained).

## Alternatives

- **Single-step campaigns** (rejected). Journeys are multi-step; industry standard. Single-step cannot model welcome series, re-engagement, or lifecycle flows.
- **No exit conditions** (rejected). Law 18 violation; excluded players continue receiving. The 2022 UK GC and 2023 Caesars incidents are the canonical examples.
- **Exit on next step only (not immediate)** (rejected). Player who self-excludes should not receive the next step even if it's already queued; immediate exit via kill-switch fan-out is required.
- **No exit_reason** (rejected). Regulator inquiry asks "why did this player exit the journey?"; without `exit_reason`, the operator cannot answer.
- **Journey without segment** (rejected). Journeys target segments; without segment, targeting is ad-hoc and unauditable.
- **DSAR erasure deletes enrollment** (rejected). Loses analytics; anonymized retention (player_uuid NULLed) preserves journey completion stats without player attribution.

## Consequences

- Journey + JourneyStep + JourneyEnrollment models.
- Multi-step execution with per-step MarketingGate check.
- 6 exit conditions (self_excluded, unsubscribed, cooling_off, terminal_step, no_consent, DSAR erasure).
- Kill-switch fan-out exits enrollment immediately (Law 18 same-transaction).
- Linter rule 202: exit_reason NOT NULL when status=exited.
- DSAR erasure anonymizes enrollment (player_uuid NULLed; journey continues for analytics).
- Open question OQ-ENG-003: journey step retry policy (retry vs advance on send-time denial; OPEN).

## Validation Plan

- **Journey enrollment test:** player joins segment; assert enrollment created; assert `current_step=1`; assert `next_step_at` correct.
- **Step execution test:** trigger step at `next_step_at`; assert MarketingGate queue-time check; assert send queued if eligible.
- **Exit condition test (self-exclusion):** player self-excludes mid-journey; assert kill-switch fan-out exits enrollment; assert `status=exited`; `exit_reason=self_excluded`.
- **Exit condition test (unsubscribe):** player unsubscribes; assert next step denied at queue-time; assert enrollment exits; `exit_reason=unsubscribed`.
- **Exit condition test (cooling-off):** player enters cooling-off; assert next step denied; enrollment exits; `exit_reason=cooling_off`.
- **Exit condition test (terminal step):** player reaches last step; assert `status=completed`; `exit_reason=terminal_step`.
- **Exit condition test (consent revoked):** player revokes consent; assert next step denied at send-time; enrollment exits; `exit_reason=no_consent`.
- **Exit condition test (DSAR erasure):** player DSAR-erases; assert enrollment `player_uuid` NULLed; assert journey continues for other players.
- **Queue-time denial test:** player suppressed at queue-time; assert send NOT queued; enrollment `status=suppressed`.
- **Send-time denial test:** cap exceeded at send-time; assert send skipped; step may retry or advance.
- **Linter rule 202 test:** grep for enrollment exits; assert all have `exit_reason` NOT NULL.
