# ADR-0021 — Execution-layer phase grouping (27 stages → 25 phases)

## Context

The original 27-stage plan had two pairs of stages that the execution layer regrouped into single phases: (a) MFA + session management and (b) recon rules + recon runs. Treating them as separate stages created artificial dependencies and inflated the phase count. Errata E3 corrected the count from 26 to 25.

## Decision

Regroup the 27 stages into 25 phases by merging the two pairs above. The merge is recorded in BLUEPRINT §5 and in errata E3. The phase table is the single authority for phase numbering. Phase 2 review (DEC-0021 narrative in traceability.md §C.3) confirms the regrouping remains valid.

## Alternatives

Alternatives considered: (a) keep 27 stages — artificial dependencies; (b) merge to 24 phases — over-merging, loses audit trail of the original stages; (c) re-number freely — breaks external references. (a) rejected for dependencies; (b) rejected for over-merging; (c) rejected for reference breakage.

## Consequences

Future stage additions or removals require a new erratum and a new phase-table version. Phase numbering is now stable; external documents may cite phase numbers without ambiguity.

## Validation Plan

Phase 2: errata E3 verified (see docs/errata/v5.2.1-verification.md). Phase 2: DEC-0021 narrative reviewed and recorded in traceability.md §C.3. Future phases: monitor for re-merge or re-split triggers.
