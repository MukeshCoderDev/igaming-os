# ADR-0145 — Operator Statements — Financial Reporting & Per-Jurisdiction Breakdown

## Context

Operator statements are the financial summary sent to operators. Operators need per-jurisdiction GGR for their own regulatory filings. Statements must reconcile against ledger. Delivery via portal or email (transactional, not marketing — bypasses MarketingGate).

The 2022 BetBright enforcement (£600K, cited in Phase 21 ADR-0134) illustrated the cost of inaccurate statements: the operator's statement showed GGR £500K lower than the ledger due to a spreadsheet formula error. The operator under-reported GGR to the regulator; the regulator's reconciliation against bank records found the discrepancy. The fine was for "inaccurate regulatory reporting" — not for the transactions, but for the reporting error. The lesson: statements must reconcile against the ledger (source of truth), not against manual exports.

The second threat is MarketingGate blocking transactional statements. Operator statements are financial reports (transactional), not marketing campaigns. If MarketingGate applies suppression/cap checks to statement delivery, operators who have unsubscribed from marketing would not receive their financial statements — a regulatory violation (operators must receive financial reporting). The cure: linter rule 209 (operator statements are transactional; not subject to MarketingGate).

**Linter rule 210:** every operator statement has `reconciliation_status`.

### Statement contents

- **GGR breakdown:** per game_class (casino, live_casino, crash, multiplayer), per jurisdiction.
- **NGR breakdown:** GGR minus deductions (bonuses, payment fees, chargeback fees).
- **Fee calculation:** rev_share_rate applied, tiered calculation if applicable.
- **Invoice total:** `total_amount_minor`.
- **Payment status:** paid/overdue/disputed.

### Per-jurisdiction breakdown

- Regulator-reportable; operator may need per-jurisdiction GGR for their own regulatory filings.
- Sourced from Phase 17 GGR read model (per-jurisdiction aggregation).
- Reconciles against Phase 11 ledger (per-jurisdiction).

### Delivery

- **Portal:** operator dashboard (self-service download).
- **Email:** PDF attachment via Phase 8 notifications.
- **Transactional send (NOT marketing)** — bypasses MarketingGate suppression/cap checks.
- **Linter rule 209:** operator statements are transactional; not subject to MarketingGate.
- Audit: delivery logged (`sent_at`, `sent_to`, `delivery_method`).

### Reconciliation

- Statement reconciles against Phase 11 ledger + Phase 17 GGR read model.
- Mismatch → alert + HALT (Phase 11 reconciliation pattern).
- **Linter rule 210:** every operator statement has `reconciliation_status`.

### Acknowledgment tracking

- Operator acknowledges receipt (portal click or email open tracking).
- `status=acknowledged`.
- Audit event.

## Alternatives

- **No statements** (rejected). Operators need financial summary; regulator-reportable.
- **Marketing channel delivery** (rejected). Transactional; not subject to suppression/caps. An unsubscribed operator must still receive financial statements.
- **No per-jurisdiction breakdown** (rejected). Operators need for regulatory filings (UK GC monthly GGR; MGA quarterly).
- **No reconciliation** (rejected). The BetBright incident showed unreconciled statements cause inaccurate regulatory reporting.
- **No acknowledgment tracking** (rejected). Cannot prove delivery; regulator asks "did the operator receive the statement?"
- **No portal delivery (email only)** (rejected). Some operators prefer portal self-service; email may be blocked by spam filters.

## Consequences

- OperatorStatement model with per-jurisdiction breakdown.
- Portal + email delivery (transactional; bypasses MarketingGate).
- Reconciliation + HALT (linter rule 210).
- Acknowledgment tracking.
- Linter rule 209: transactional bypass (not subject to MarketingGate).
- Open question OQ-COMM-005: statement delivery method (portal vs email vs both; OPEN).
- Open question OQ-COMM-006: invoice currency (per-operator vs per-jurisdiction; OPEN).

## Validation Plan

- **Statement generation test:** generate statement for period; assert GGR/NGR breakdown per game_class + per jurisdiction.
- **Per-jurisdiction breakdown test:** statement with UK + MGA + NJ; assert per-jurisdiction GGR correct.
- **Reconciliation test:** generate statement; run reconciliation; assert `reconciliation_status=matched`; inject mismatch; assert `mismatched` + HALT.
- **Delivery test (portal):** operator downloads statement from portal; assert `status=sent`; assert delivery logged.
- **Delivery test (email):** statement emailed as PDF; assert `status=sent`; assert delivery logged.
- **Transactional bypass test (linter rule 209):** operator unsubscribed from marketing; attempt statement delivery; assert NOT blocked by MarketingGate (transactional bypass).
- **Acknowledgment tracking test:** operator acknowledges via portal; assert `status=acknowledged`; assert audit event.
