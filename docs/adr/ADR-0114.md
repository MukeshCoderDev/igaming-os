# ADR-0114 — Player Appeal Workflow & Reversal

## Context

Players can appeal punitive actions (regulator-mandated in some jurisdictions). UK GC requires a complaint/appeal process for license holders; MGA requires dispute resolution; Ontario AGCO requires player recourse. Even where not explicitly mandated, an appeal workflow is operational best practice — it reduces regulatory complaints and provides a paper trail for disputes.

The appeal workflow must respect two constraints:

1. **Tipping-off prohibition still applies.** If the punitive action was triggered by a SAR filing (ADR-0111), the player cannot be told about the SAR. The appeal review must not reveal the SAR existence. The player sees "your account was frozen due to a compliance review" — not "your account was frozen because we filed a SAR."

2. **Irreversible actions cannot be appealed to reversal.** SE enforcement (se_block) and account closure (account_closure) are terminal (ADR-0113). A player can appeal the decision (the appeal is reviewed), but the appeal cannot reverse the action. If the appeal is upheld (the action was wrong), the player may receive compensation or an apology, but the SE/closure stands. This is regulator-mandated for SE (Phase 14 ADR-0085).

### FSM

```
submitted → under_review → upheld (action stands; no reversal)
                         ↘ overturned (action reversed via new PunitiveAction)
                         ↘ rejected (appeal dismissed; action stands)
```

- `submitted → under_review`: Appeal submitted by player; compliance officer assigned for review.
- `under_review → upheld`: The original punitive action was correct; the appeal is denied. The action stands. The player is notified of the decision (NOT of any underlying SAR).
- `under_review → overturned`: The original punitive action was incorrect; the appeal is granted. The action is reversed (per ADR-0113 reversal rules). The player is notified + may receive compensation.
- `under_review → rejected`: The appeal was submitted outside the appeal window or is procedurally invalid. The action stands.

### Appeal window

Configurable per jurisdiction (e.g., 30 days default; Q1-blocked for specific windows; OQ-RISK-005). The `JurisdictionPort` (Phase 14) returns the appeal window for the player's jurisdiction. Appeals submitted after the window are auto-rejected.

### Review workflow

1. Compliance officer reviews the appeal + the original punitive action + the underlying RiskCase.
2. Decision:
   - **Uphold:** The original action was correct. Appeal denied. Player notified (without revealing SAR if applicable).
   - **Overturn:** The original action was incorrect. Action reversed (per ADR-0113). Player notified + may receive compensation.
   - **Reject:** Procedural rejection (outside window; invalid appeal).
3. **Separation of duties:** The reviewer (`reviewed_by`) must NOT be the same person who approved the original punitive action (`approved_by` on PunitiveAction). This prevents self-review of one's own decisions.

### Tipping-off prohibition

- If the punitive action was triggered by a SAR (case links to sar_case_uuid), the appeal review must NOT reveal the SAR to the player.
- Player notification template: "Your appeal regarding [action_type] has been [upheld/overturned]. [Decision justification]." — no mention of SAR, MLRO, FIU, or suspicion.
- Linter rule 155 (tipping-off prohibition) extends to appeal notifications: grep for appeal notification templates referencing SAR-related strings. Zero findings required.

### Reversal on overturn

If the appeal is overturned, the punitive action is reversed per ADR-0113 reversal rules:
- `account_freeze` → unfreeze (new PunitiveAction)
- `bonus_clawback` → re-award (Phase 11 reversal-of-reversal; new ledger entry)
- `payment_block` → unblock (new PunitiveAction)
- `se_block` → CANNOT reverse (regulator-mandated terminal; appeal may uphold or overturn the decision but cannot reverse the SE)
- `account_closure` → CANNOT reverse (terminal)

For irreversible actions, an overturned appeal may result in compensation (ledger credit) or an apology, but the action itself stands.

### Audit

Every appeal submission + review is audited (Phase 7). Event types:
- `appeal.submitted` (with punitive_action_uuid, submitted_reason)
- `appeal.reviewed` (with reviewed_by, decision, decision_justification)
- `appeal.overturned` (with reversal_punitive_action_uuid)

### Notification

Player notified of appeal decision (NOT of SAR — tipping-off prohibition still applies). Notification template is generic: "Your appeal has been [upheld/overturned/rejected]." No SAR-related language.

## Alternatives

- **No appeals** (rejected). Regulator-mandated in some jurisdictions; operational best practice everywhere.
- **Auto-approve appeals** (rejected). Compliance review required; auto-approve would undermine punitive actions.
- **Same reviewer as original approver** (rejected). No separation of duties; self-review risk.
- **Reversal of SE** (rejected). Regulator-mandated terminal (Phase 14 ADR-0085). Reversal would violate SE-survives-erasure.
- **Reversal of account closure** (rejected). Terminal by definition.
- **No appeal window** (rejected). Without a window, players could appeal years later; cases would never close.
- **Appeal window not configurable** (rejected). Different jurisdictions have different windows (UK 30 days; MGA 60 days; etc.). Q1-blocked for specific values.

## Consequences

- Appeal model with 5-state FSM.
- Appeal window configurable per jurisdiction (Q1-blocked; OQ-RISK-005).
- Separation of duties: reviewer != original approver.
- Tipping-off prohibition extends to appeal notifications (linter rule 155).
- Reversal on overturn for reversible actions; irreversible actions stand (compensation instead).
- Every appeal audited (Phase 7).
- Linter rule 153: Appeal model exists with FSM + separation of duties.

## Validation Plan

- **Appeal submission test:** player submits appeal within window; assert status `submitted`.
- **Appeal window test:** player submits appeal after window; assert auto-rejection.
- **Review test (uphold):** compliance officer upholds; assert action stands; assert player notified.
- **Review test (overturn):** compliance officer overturns; assert reversal PunitiveAction created; assert player notified.
- **Separation of duties test:** original approver attempts to review appeal; assert refusal.
- **Tipping-off test:** appeal on SAR-triggered action; assert player notification contains no SAR-related language.
- **Irreversibility test (se_block):** overturn appeal on se_block; assert SE stands; assert no reversal PunitiveAction created.
- **Reversal test (account_freeze):** overturn appeal on account_freeze; assert unfreeze PunitiveAction created.
- **Linter rule 153 test:** grep for Appeal model; assert FSM + separation of duties fields.
