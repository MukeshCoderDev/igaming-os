# ADR-0015 — RBAC with delegation chains

## Context

Customer pain (Pain-REQ-0012) demands role-based access control with delegation, so that a senior user can temporarily delegate a subset of their permissions to another user. Simple role assignment is insufficient.

## Decision

Adopt RBAC with delegation chains: a user may delegate a subset of their permissions to another user, with a chain-depth limit (default 3). Delegations are revocable. The authorization check walks the delegation chain at request time.

## Alternatives

Alternatives considered: (a) flat RBAC without delegation — doesn't meet Pain-REQ-0012; (b) ABAC (attribute-based) — more flexible but harder to audit; (c) delegated OAuth scopes — OAuth scopes are coarse. (a) rejected for insufficient capability; (b) rejected for auditability; (c) rejected for coarseness.

## Consequences

Delegation chain walks are O(depth); performance impact is small at the default depth limit but grows with depth. Revocation must propagate; a revoked delegation must immediately invalidate downstream delegations.

## Validation Plan

Phase 5: implement; verify that a delegation of depth 4 is rejected. Phase 5: verify revocation propagates within 1s. Phase 7: load-test authorization checks at 5k req/s.
